What to check before you order

CSSBuy product links and seller options

Record the original Taobao, Weidian or 1688 listing alongside the exact option label. When comparing listings, check whether the price covers one piece, a pack or the whole set. On wholesale listings, confirm the minimum order quantity. Keep a screenshot of the chosen option if colours or sizes share similar names.

CSSBuy sizing guide for shoes and clothing

Measure something you already wear, then compare it with the seller size chart in centimetres. A flat chest width and a full chest circumference are different measurements. For US, UK and EU shoe sizes, confirm whether the chart gives foot length or insole length before choosing.

CSSBuy QC photos and warehouse measurements

Check that each photo belongs to the option you ordered. For shoes, compare both size labels and inspect the toe box and stitching. For clothing, ask for a tape measure in the frame and a close-up of the print or seam. If something differs from the listing, check the seller’s return window before forwarding the parcel.

CSSBuy shipping estimate and parcel cost

Keep separate entries for the item subtotal, domestic shipping, agent fees and international delivery. Use the packed weight and parcel dimensions when comparing eligible routes. If a carrier uses volumetric weight, a bulky shoe box can affect the quote. Check import costs for the destination separately.

A shortlist you can check later

Before paying

  • Save the seller link, option label, listed currency and date checked.
  • Write down one fit measurement to confirm and note any missing size information.
  • Check stock for the selected option and whether domestic delivery is included.

Before forwarding

  • Compare the warehouse photos with your notes and resolve visible discrepancies.
  • Check the final parcel dimensions and available routes for your destination.
  • Review the total with any packing charges and destination charges that apply.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

A CSSBuy spreadsheet is a product shortlist used when shopping through CSSBuy. It typically groups seller links by category so you can compare listings, sizes and available options.

How do I check a CSSBuy product link?

Open the original seller listing and confirm that the selected size, colour and quantity match your shortlist. If the link is unavailable, ask for a current listing before paying.

What should I look for in QC photos?

Compare the warehouse photos with the option you ordered. Look for the size label, colour, visible condition and requested measurements. For close details, ask for a sharper photo or another angle.

How do I estimate shipping for a CSSBuy parcel?

Use your destination, parcel weight and package dimensions to compare eligible shipping routes in the agent’s estimator. Add the item price, domestic delivery and applicable fees to plan the total.

Does this page link directly to CSSBuy?

The category links open Hipobuyfind.com. Check the destination and the original seller listing before using a product link with your chosen shopping agent.
